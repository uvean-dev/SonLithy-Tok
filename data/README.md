# Data Policy

- Do not commit large datasets, training corpora, downloaded archives, or derived
  corpus files to Git.
- Maintain a reviewed manifest of data sources, versions or snapshots, licenses,
  provenance, and permitted uses for every corpus used in experiments.
- Public availability does not automatically grant permission for every use,
  transformation, or redistribution.
- Check exact and near-duplicate content and split data to avoid leakage between
  training, tuning, and evaluation.
- Do not publish datasets or their provenance in this public repository until their
  redistribution and attribution requirements have been reviewed.
